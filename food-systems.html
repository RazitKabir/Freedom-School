A Youth-Led Vision for Kalamazoo

Healthy food, clean water, shared knowledge, and opportunity for every neighborhood.

Scholars envision a Kalamazoo where everyone can access healthy, affordable, and culturally meaningful food. Young people should also have a voice in decisions about food, farming, gardens, land, water, and neighborhoods.

What Scholars Notice

Community strengths and opportunities

Kalamazoo Already Has

  • Restaurants and grocery stores
  • Farms, gardens, parks, and green spaces
  • Urban and rural communities
  • Hospitals and health-care services
  • People who know how to grow and prepare food

Kalamazoo Needs More

  • Healthy grocery options and fresh vegetables
  • Gardens, farms, greenhouses, and supplies
  • Clean and safe water access
  • Food education and business opportunities
  • Support for people experiencing homelessness
  • Cleaner neighborhoods with less litter

Projects We Can Start

From ideas to community action

01

Youth Garden & Farm Stand

Grow vegetables, herbs, flowers, or fruit while learning teamwork, marketing, customer service, and healthy eating.

02

Food and Agriculture Learning Days

Lead workshops about gardening, cooking, composting, food waste, food careers, and starting a food business.

03

Grow & Share Program

Grow extra food with schools, churches, farms, and families and share it with shelters, elders, food pantries, and community refrigerators.

04

Clean & Green Kalamazoo

Choose an area to clean, plant, improve, and transform into a healthier community gathering space.

Our Message to Kalamazoo

Young people are not only the future. Their ideas matter now.

Scholars notice food insecurity, unequal access, litter, empty land, and neighborhoods that need more resources. They also see possibilities: gardens, youth-run farm stands, shared food, stronger skills, and healthier communities.
