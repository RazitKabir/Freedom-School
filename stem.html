The Civic Design Studio

How STEM scholars learn and lead

The pathway follows the Commonfolk Republic developmental arc: scholars build shared knowledge, investigate systems, create solutions, and grow into civic stewards.

01

Foundations

Learn STEM vocabulary, safety, tools, teamwork, and how community systems connect.

02

Exploration

Use walking tours, interviews, mapping, observation, and research to identify patterns and challenges.

03

Application

Create prototypes, experiments, models, maps, digital products, and community recommendations.

04

Mastery

Present evidence, improve designs, lead teams, and help guide future Master Plan projects.

What Scholars Can Study

Every community system becomes a learning laboratory

Kalamazoo Systems to Explore

  • Transportation, roads, sidewalks, and accessibility
  • Housing, buildings, public spaces, and land use
  • Water, energy, waste, climate, and green infrastructure
  • Schools, libraries, digital access, and learning technologies
  • Health, wellness, safety, and community care

Questions Scholars Can Ask

  • Who can access this system and who is left out?
  • What evidence shows that the system is working?
  • Where are the risks, barriers, or points of waste?
  • What can be redesigned to make the system safer and fairer?
  • How can technology support people without replacing community voice?

Scholars in Action

Building a Sustainable Smart City Together

Scholars worked as a team to design and build a model community that connects transportation, public safety, healthcare, government, parks, housing, and sustainable infrastructure. The project turns STEM learning into civic design and shows how young people can contribute ideas to the Youth-Led Master Plan and the Commonfolk Republic vision.

Young people are researchers, inventors, builders, and civic designers.
