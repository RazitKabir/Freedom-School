ABOUT US

Our Vision

Freedom Schools Kalamazoo envisions a beloved community where every child develops a lifelong love of learning, becomes a confident leader, and has opportunities to create positive social change.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower scholars through culturally responsive literacy, civic design, leadership development, STEM, arts, human rights, food systems, and community engagement.

Our Director

Remi Harrington

Director

Welcome to Freedom Schools Kalamazoo. We believe young people are not only preparing for the future—they are capable of helping shape it today. Our work creates spaces where scholars can read, question, imagine, design, and lead alongside their community.

Our program connects literacy, creativity, civic learning, and community action. By trusting scholars as researchers and designers, we help them build the confidence and skills to contribute meaningfully to Kalamazoo and the wider world.
